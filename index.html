Larp City is a free Roblox tycoon about influencer and hustle culture. Pick a career, grow an audience from your bedroom, and blow the lot on supercars, penthouses and iced chains.

Everyone in Mayflex is building something. Mostly it's a ring light. You pick one of five careers: stream on Glitch as an Influencer, dropship phone cases as a Hustler, record for SoundClown as an Artist, mog strangers as a Mogger, or find out what's really going on at the Fringe Substation as a Truther.

You earn by playing live minigames at your home setup, then put the money back in: a better mic, a second monitor, a Stream Deck. Every upgrade pays more, keeps money coming in while you're out, and opens up gigs across the island, from a borrowed mic at Mega Mart to a sold-out night at Sticky Floors.

Then there's the question of who you want to be. Rug pulls and fake jawline courses pay brilliantly, right up until a Truther exposes you. Stay clean and you could hit Wholesome 100. Go the other way and you're the Final Boss.

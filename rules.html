Rules & FAQ

House rules

Break them and you'll be kicked or banned. Appeals go through the Discord.

Notice

  1. Follow the Roblox Community Standards.

    Everything in the Roblox rules applies in Larp City too.

  2. No VDM.

    Don't run players over or ram their vehicles on purpose.

  3. No spamming.

    Don't flood chat, the air horn or server announcements.

    Staff decisions are final in game. Appeal on the Discord if you think a mistake was made.

Questions

Is Larp City free to play?

Yes. Game passes like 2x Cash and VIP are optional. Most extras in the Store can be bought with in-game cash too.

Can I play on mobile, console or PC?

Yes. The game supports keyboard, touch screens and controllers.

How many saves can I have?

Four save slots, each with its own career, money and stuff. The +4 Save Slots pass gives you eight.

Can I change my career?

Your career is picked when you make a save. Once you reach 10,000 Clout you can add a second career as a side hustle, and swap which one is your main.

How do I make money fast?

Upgrade your home setup, do your daily goals, and save your gigs for Happy Hour (double pay for 5 minutes every 40). Check the wiki page for your career.

What happens if I get Exposed?

You lose Rep. Keep your Heat low by laying off shady moves, or post an Apology Video. See Heat on the wiki.

How do I report a player or a bug?

Open a ticket in the Discord with a screenshot or clip, and steps to reproduce bugs.

Rules & FAQ

Keep the city fun for everyone. Breaking rules can lead to a kick or ban.

Server rules

  1. Follow the Roblox Community Standards.

    Everything in the Roblox rules applies in Larp City too.

  2. No RDM or VDM.

    Don't attack players or run them over with vehicles without a roleplay reason.

  3. Stay in character.

    Keep out-of-character chat to a minimum and use "OOC:" when you need it.

  4. No exploiting or cheating.

    Using exploits, glitches or third-party tools is a permanent ban.

  5. Don't abuse job tools.

    Police, medic and other job tools are for roleplay only.

  6. Be respectful.

    No harassment, hate speech, bullying or targeting other players.

  7. No fail RP.

    Don't do things your character couldn't realistically do, or ignore what's happening in a scene.

  8. Listen to staff.

    Staff decisions are final in game. Appeal on the Discord if you think a mistake was made.

FAQ

Is Larp City free to play?

Yes! Larp City is completely free. Some optional game passes are available to support development.

Can I play on mobile, console or PC?

Yes, Larp City supports PC, mobile, tablet and controller.

How do I report a player?

Use the in-game report button or open a ticket in the Discord with a screenshot or clip.

How do I become staff?

Staff applications are posted in the Discord when they open. Keep an eye on the announcements channel.

I found a bug. What do I do?

Report it in the Discord bug-reports channel with steps to reproduce it.

Can I help write the wiki?

Yes! Send your edits or new articles to staff on the Discord.
